About Me

I'm Dr. Sahithyaa Raghu

Counselling Psychologist and RCI-Licensed Therapist with over 17 years of experience working with individuals, families, children, professionals, and communities.

More than titles or qualifications, my work is about listening without judgment and creating a space where people feel emotionally safe enough to be real—sometimes for the first time in a long while.

How I can support you

You don't need to fit into a label or diagnosis to seek support.

People come to counselling for many different reasons—and all of them are valid.

Individual Counselling

At times, emotions feel too much to manage alone.

This is a calm, compassionate space to pause, breathe, and speak freely—without judgment or pressure.

We gently explore stress, anxiety, emotional exhaustion, low mood, self-doubt, and life changes, always at a pace that feels safe for you.

Child & Adolescent Support

Children and young people often feel deeply, even when words are hard to find.

This space helps them feel seen, understood, and emotionally safe.

Parents are supported too—with patience, clarity, and reassurance—so no one feels alone during these tender stages.

Couple & Family Counselling

When relationships feel strained or connection feels distant, it can be deeply painful.

Counselling offers a respectful, caring space to slow down, listen with kindness, and rebuild understanding—together, step by step.

Psychological Assessments

Sometimes clarity brings relief.

Assessments are offered gently and thoughtfully to better understand emotional, behavioural, or developmental concerns—only when helpful, and always explained with care.

Corporate Wellness & Consulting

Work shapes our emotional lives in quiet ways.

Over time, stress, pressure, and constant expectations can take a toll—even when everything appears fine on the surface.

These services support organisations in caring for the emotional wellbeing of their people, with compassion and respect.

This includes supporting mental health at work, addressing stress and burnout, building psychologically safe leadership and teams, and gently supporting women returning from career breaks or maternity through confidence-building, emotionally supportive reintegration.

Support is offered through counselling, conversations, workshops, and guidance—always confidential, thoughtful, and non-judgmental.

What counselling is like here

Many people worry about what therapy will be like.

Here's what usually helps to know:

We begin with listening

You decide what to share and when. Silence is welcome too.

We understand together

There is no pressure to "fix" things quickly or perform progress.

We work collaboratively

You are always part of the decisions. Your voice matters.

We pause and close thoughtfully

Counselling is not endless. We review, integrate, and close when you feel ready.

You remain in control throughout.

A Note on Safety, Ethics & Confidentiality

Your privacy and dignity are deeply respected here. Sessions are strictly confidential.

Your information is never shared without consent. Exceptions apply only in rare, legally mandated situations.

This is a non-judgmental, inclusive, and respectful space. Your story is treated with care. It is never discussed casually or without purpose.

Beyond the clinic

Community & Social Outreach

Mental health care should not be a privilege.

Alongside private practice, I am actively involved in community-based mental health work through Sankalpam Mind & Wellness Foundation—an initiative focused on emotional wellbeing, mental health awareness, and accessible psychological support, especially for underserved populations.

This outreach work reflects a core belief of this practice: Healing is both personal and collective, and care should reach beyond walls and labels.

Before You Reach Out

If something feels heavy, confusing, or quietly exhausting—and you're considering support—that is reason enough. You don't need to be in crisis or know exactly what's wrong.
