Whom Am I and How I Work

I'm Dr. Sahithyaa Raghu

Counselling Psychologist and RCI-Licensed Therapist with over 17 years of experience working with individuals, families, children, professionals, and communities.

This space is not about being analysed or judged. It is about being listened to with care, understood with respect, and supported at a pace that feels right for you.

My work is grounded in evidence-based psychology, ethical practice, and a deep belief that healing begins when people feel emotionally safe enough to be real.

You are welcome to bring uncertainty, questions, or silence. We begin wherever you are.

Professional background :

Doctorate in Psychology • Doctorate in Counselling and Organizational Behaviour. MSc Counselling & Psychotherapy • MBA (HR) • MSW • LLB • RCI License . Human Rights

  • Emotional numbness

What Counselling is Like Here

We begin with listening

You decide what to share and when. Silence is welcome too.

We understand together

There is no pressure to "fix" things quickly or perform progress.

We work collaboratively

You are always part of the decisions. Your voice matters.

We pause and close thoughtfully

Counselling is not endless. We review, integrate, and close when you feel ready.

You remain in control throughout.
