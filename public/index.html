About Serenity Circle

A sanctuary for your mind, body, and heart.

At Serenity Circle, we believe healing is not linear. It moves in waves, circles, and slow openings. Our role is to walk beside you—never ahead of you—as you untangle stress, anxiety, grief, and inner conflict.

Our therapists combine modern psychological practices with mindfulness, somatic awareness, and gentle lifestyle changes. Every session is tailored to who you are, where you are, and how you want to feel.

Safe & inclusive

LGBTQIA+ affirming, trauma-informed, culturally sensitive spaces where all identities are honored.

Holistic approach

We support your emotional, mental, physical, and spiritual wellness as one interconnected system.

Sustainable change

No quick fixes—just grounded tools you can use in everyday life, long after the session ends.

What we offer

Counseling services designed around real life.

Whether you’re feeling overwhelmed, stuck, or simply ready for change, there’s a space here for you.

Individual therapy

One-on-one sessions that help you unpack emotions, patterns, and stories at a pace that feels right for you.

Couples & relationships

Create space for honest communication, repair ruptures, and rebuild trust and connection.

Group circles
